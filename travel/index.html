About this archive

City names describe nearby geographic groups, not verified landmarks. Some video coordinates report an accuracy of 3–7 km. Ungrouped photographs remain available in the archive.

Coastlines: Natural Earth, public domain. Original files remain unchanged. Collage plates are artistic interpretations generated from the source photographs, with paper, halftone and pencil treatments.
